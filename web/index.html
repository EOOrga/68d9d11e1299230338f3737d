Daily operations without cloud overhead

The Android app is built around repeated operational work: create tasks, review patient follow-ups, track revenue, and keep an audit trail of important changes.

T

Task command list

Capture owners, priorities, and due times for the actions that keep the day moving.

P

Patient records

Save record codes, next actions, status, notes, and review flags for local follow-up.

R

Revenue analytics

Separate collected and pending revenue so management can see the current position quickly.

A

Audit history

Every important local action is reflected in an audit log for review and accountability.

Designed for local control

The app does not use accounts, analytics, advertising identifiers, cloud sync, network APIs, or Android runtime permissions. Records remain in local app storage until the app data is removed or the app is uninstalled.

1. Add records

Create task, patient, and revenue entries directly on the phone.

2. Review status

Use summaries and lists to find overdue actions, flagged records, and pending revenue.

3. Keep accountability

Audit events provide a private timeline of updates made inside the app.
