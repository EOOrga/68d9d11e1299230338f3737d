App name
DECLOQUEMENT ERIC PIERRE

Short description
Manage tasks, patient records, audits, and revenue on one private Android workspace.

Full description
DECLOQUEMENT ERIC PIERRE helps small operations teams keep daily business work organized without accounts or cloud services. Track tasks, patient records, revenue items, and audit activity from a focused phone interface.

The app is designed for quick repeated use during the working day. Add operational actions, review patient follow-ups, monitor collected and pending revenue, and keep a clear local audit trail of changes.
